# H.R. 4867

> To amend title 3, United States Code, to permit an objection to the certificate of the electoral votes of a State to be received by the Senate and the House of Representatives if the objection is signed by either a Senator or a Member of the House of Representatives.

## § 1. Permitting Objection to Certificate of Electoral Votes to be Received if Signed by Senator or Member of the House of Representatives

The fifth sentence of section 15 of title 3, United States Code, is amended by striking “and shall be signed by at least one Senator and one Member of the House of Representatives” and inserting “and shall be signed by at least one Senator or Member of the House of Representatives”.
