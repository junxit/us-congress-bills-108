# H.R. 4296

> To reauthorize and revise the Child Care Access Means Parents in School Program (CCAMPIS).

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “&short-title1;”.

## § 2. Reauthorization and revision of CCAMPIS

- **(a)** *Minimum grant* Section 419N(b)(2)(B) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1070e(b)(2)(B)) is amended by striking “$10,000” and inserting “$30,000”.
- **(b)** *Eligible institutions* Section 419N(b)(4) of such Act is amended by striking “$350,000” and inserting “$250,000”.
- **(c)** *Definition of low income student* Section 419N(b)(7) of such Act is amended by striking “a student who is eligible to receive” and inserting “a student whose family income makes the student eligible to receive”.
- **(d)** *Authorization of appropriations* Section 419N(g) of such Act is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “$45,000,000” and inserting “$75,000,000”;
  - **(2)** by striking “1999” and inserting “2005”; and
  - **(3)** by striking “4 succeeding” and inserting “5 succeeding”.
