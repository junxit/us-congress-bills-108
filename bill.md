# H.R. 3959

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to authorize the Secretary of Homeland Security, acting through the Under Secretary for Border and Transportation Security, to provide air marshal training to law enforcement personnel of foreign countries.

## § 1. Federal air marshal training

Section 44917 of title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(d)** *Training for foreign law enforcement personnel*
>   - **(1)** *In general* The Under Secretary may direct the Federal Air Marshal Service to provide air marshal training to law enforcement personnel of foreign countries.
>   - **(2)** *Fees* The Under Secretary shall establish reasonable fees and charges to pay expenses incurred in carrying out this subsection. Money collected under this subsection shall be credited to the account in the Treasury from which the expenses were incurred and shall be available to the Under Secretary for those expenses.
