# H.R. 4828

> To direct the Consumer Product Safety Commission to issue a rule banning children’s toys containing mercury.

## § 1. Toys containing mercury as banned hazardous products

- **(a)** *Product banned* Not later than 6 months after the date of enactment of this Act, the Consumer Product Safety Commission shall, pursuant to section 8 of the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2057), promulgate a rule under section 9 of such Act, declaring mercury-containing toys to be banned hazardous products under such Act.
- **(b)** *Definition of mercury-containing toys* As used in this Act, the term “mercury-containing toy” means any toy or other product that contains mercury and is designed primarily for, or marketed primarily to children.
