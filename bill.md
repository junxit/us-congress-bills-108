# H. RES. 621

> Supporting the goals and ideals of National Correctional Officers and Employees Week.

## Preamble

- Whereas the operation of correctional facilities represents a crucial component of the criminal justice system of the United States;
- Whereas correctional personnel play a vital role in protecting the rights of the public to be safeguarded from criminal activity;
- Whereas correctional personnel are responsible for the care, custody, and dignity of the human beings charged to their care;
- Whereas correctional personnel work under demanding circumstances and face danger in their daily work lives; and
- Whereas the week beginning May 2, 2004, has been designated as National Correctional Officers and Employees Week: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** supports the goals and ideals of National Correctional Officers and Employees Week; and
- **(2)** honors all correctional officers and employees for their service to their communities, States, and the Nation.
