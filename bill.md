# H.R. 3721

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to repeal the essential air service local participation program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “&short-title1;”.

## § 2. Repeal of Eas Local Participation Program

Section 41747 of title 49, United States Code, and the item relating to such section in the analysis for subchapter II of chapter 417 of such title, is repealed.
