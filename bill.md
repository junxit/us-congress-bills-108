# H.R. 4688

> To amend the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to reauthorize the Chesapeake Bay Program.

## § 1. Reauthorization of Chesapeake Bay Program

Section 117(j) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1267(j)) is amended by striking “2005” and inserting “2010”.
