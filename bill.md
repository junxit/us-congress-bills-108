# H.R. 4071

> To extend the time within which claims may be filed under the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund.

## § 1. Extension of time for filing claims under September 11th Victim Compensation Fund

Section 405(a)(3) of the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund of 2001 (49 U.S.C. 40101 note) is amended by striking “2” and inserting “3”.
