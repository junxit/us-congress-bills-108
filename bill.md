# H.R. 3772

> To include Nelson County and Franklin County, Virginia, in the Appalachian region for purposes of the programs of the Appalachian Regional Commission.

## § 1. Inclusion of Nelson County and Franklin County, Virginia, in the Appalachian region

Section 14102(a)(1)(L) of title 40, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “Franklin,” after “Floyd,”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “Nelson,” after “Montgomery,”.
