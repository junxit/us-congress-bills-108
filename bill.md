# H.R. 4465

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to extend loan forgiveness for certain loans to certified or licensed teachers.

## § 1. Expansion of new borrower eligibility for student loan forgiveness for teachers

- **(a)** *FFEL loans* Section 428J(b) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1078–10(b)) is amended by striking “October 1, 1998,” and inserting “October 17, 1986,”.
- **(b)** *Direct loans* Section 460(b)(1) of such Act (20 U.S.C. 1087j(b)(1)) is amended by striking “October 1, 1998,” and inserting “October 17, 1986,”.
