# H.R. 4163

> To provide for a greater number of members on certain combined Farm Service Agency county committees.

## § 1. Increased membership of certain combined Farm Service Agency county committees

Section 8(b)(5)(B) of the Soil Conservation and Domestic Allotment Act (16 U.S.C. 590h(b)(5)(B)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(vi)** *Membership of combined farm service agency county committees* Notwithstanding the numerical limitations of clause (ii), a county committee that results from the combination or consolidation of county, area, or local committees representing 3 or more counties shall consist of not fewer than 3 nor more than the applicable number of members who meet the requirements of clause (ii). For purposes of the preceding sentence, the applicable number is the sum of 1 and twice the number of counties represented by the members of the committee.
