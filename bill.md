# H.R. 3910

> To amend the Transportation Equity Act for the 21st Century to modify a high priority project in the State of Michigan.

## § 1. High priority project, Michigan

Item number 820 of the table contained in section 1602 of the Transportation Equity Act for the 21st Century (112 Stat. 287) is amended by striking “Conduct” and all that follows through “interchange” and inserting “Conduct a transportation needs study and make improvements to 1-75 interchanges in the Grayling area”.
