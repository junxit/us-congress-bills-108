# H.R. 4675

> To amend the Caribbean Basin Economic Recovery Act to increase the quantity of T-shirts that may receive duty-free treatment during the 1-year period beginning October 1, 2003.

## § 1. Increase in quantity of T-shirts eligible for duty-free treatment

Section 213(b)(2)(A)(iii)(IV)(cc) of the Caribbean Basin Economic Recovery Act (19 U.S.C. 2703(b)(2)(A)(iii)(IV)(cc) is amended by striking “10,000,000” and inserting “12,000,000”.
