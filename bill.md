# H.R. 4012

> To amend the District of Columbia College Access Act of 1999 to permanently authorize the public school and private school tuition assistance programs established under the Act.

## § 1. Permanent Authorization of Tuition Assistance Programs

- **(a)** *Public School Program* Section 3(i) of the District of Columbia College Access Act of 1999 (sec. 38—2702(i), D.C. Official Code) is amended by striking “each of the five succeeding fiscal years” and inserting “each succeeding fiscal year”.
- **(b)** *Private School Program* Section 5(f) of such Act (sec. 38—2704(f), D.C. Official Code) is amended by striking “each of the five succeeding fiscal years” and inserting “each succeeding fiscal year”.
