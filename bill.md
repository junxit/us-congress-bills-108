# H.R. 5201

> To suspend temporarily the duty on electron guns for cathode ray tubes (CRT’s) with a high definition television screen aspect ratio of 16:9, and for other purposes.

  - **(3)** *Plasma display panels* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by inserting in numerical sequence the following new heading:
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
