# H.R. 5091

> To provide a technical correction to the Federal preemption of State or local laws concerning the markings and identification of imitation or toy firearms entering into interstate commerce.

## § 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the “Toy Gun Marking Improvement Act”.

## § 2. Preemption of State or local laws concerning the markings and identification of imitation or toy firearms

Section 4 of the Federal Energy Management Improvement Act of 1988 (15 U.S.C. 5001) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (c), by adding at the end: “Such term does include any toy replica of an antique firearm developed prior to 1898.”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (g), by amending paragraph (1) to read as follows:
