# H.R. 4767

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to triple the amount of the credit allowed for basic research.

## § 1. Increase in amount of credit for basic research

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (2) of section 41(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to general rule for credit for increasing research activities) is amended by striking “20 percent” and inserting “60 percent”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to taxable years beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.
