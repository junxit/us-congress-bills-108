# H.R. 4326

> To authorize an outpatient clinic to be established in Denton, Texas, for the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Authorization of Department of Veterans Affairs outpatient clinic in Denton, Texas

The Secretary of Veterans Affairs may take such steps as necessary to establish an outpatient clinic in Denton, Texas.
