# H. RES. 749

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to limit the maximum voting time.

That the last sentence of clause 2(a) of rule XX of the House of Representatives is amended by inserting “and, except by unanimous consent or mutual agreement of the majority and minority leaders, the maximum time shall be 17 minutes” before the period at the end.
