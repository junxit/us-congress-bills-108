# H. RES. 588

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to repeal clause 3 of rule XXI relating to transportation obligation limitations.

That clause 3 of rule XXI of the Rules of the House of Representatives (relating to transportation obligation limitations) is repealed and the succeeding clauses are redesignated accordingly.
