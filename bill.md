# H.R. 4363

> To facilitate self-help housing homeownership opportunities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “&short-title1;”.

## § 2. Assistance for self-help housing providers

Paragraph (1) of section 11(b) of the Housing Opportunity Program Extension Act of 1996 (42 U.S.C. 12805 note) is amended by striking “dwelling” and inserting “dwellings”.
