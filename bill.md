# H.R. 4537

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on Pigment Yellow 154.

## § 1. Pigment Yellow 154

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.32.18 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by striking “12/31/2002” and inserting “12/31/2006”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after January 1, 2003.
