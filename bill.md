## § 3. Parole authority for certain persons

The United States Parole Commission created by the amendments made by this Act shall also have jurisdiction over the parole of persons whose parole was governed by the Parole Commission Phase-Out Act of 1996 or section 11231 of Public Law 105–33, and shall exercise parole authority with respect to those persons under the amendments made by this Act.
