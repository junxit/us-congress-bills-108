# H.R. 4970

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to repeal the security screening opt-out program for airport operators.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Passenger Security Act of 2004”.

## § 2. Security screening opt-out program

Section 44920 of title 49, United States Code, and the item relating to such section in the analysis for chapter 449 of such title, are repealed.
