# H.R. 4836

> To name the Department of Veterans Affairs medical center in Amarillo, Texas, as the “Thomas E. Creek Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”.

## § 1. Name of Department of Veterans Affairs medical center in Amarillo, Texas

The Department of Veterans Affairs medical center in Amarillo, Texas, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Thomas E. Creek Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”. Any reference to that medical center in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be considered to be a reference to the Thomas E. Creek Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center.
