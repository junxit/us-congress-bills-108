# H.R. 5376

> For the relief of Nancy P. Gilbert.

## § 1. Posthumous promotion

Nancy P. Gilbert, formerly of Marietta, Georgia, is hereby posthumously promoted to the position of GS–1810-13 supervisory investigator in the Atlanta District Office of the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, effective as of July 2, 2001. This section shall not be taken into account for any pay or other personnel management purposes.
