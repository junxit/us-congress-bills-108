# H.R. 5359

> To amend the Tariff Act of 1930 to allow for improved administration of new shipper administrative reviews.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “New Shipper Review Amendment Act of 2004”.

## § 2. Repeal of new shipper bonding privileges

Section 751(a)(2)(B) of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 1675(a)(2)(B)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking clause (iii); and
- **(2)** by redesignating clause (iv) as clause (iii).
