# H.R. 4304

> To amend the Medicare Prescription Drug, Improvement, and Modernization Act of 2003 to eliminate overpayments to health maintenance organizations and other private plans under part C of title XVIII of the Social Security Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “&short-title1;”.

## § 2. Elimination of ma regional plan stabilization fund (slush fund)

Subsection (e) of section 1858 of the Social Security Act, as added by section 221(c) of the Medicare Prescription Drug, Improvement, and Modernization Act of 2003 (Public Law 108–173), is repealed.

## § 3. Phase-in to payment at 100 percent of fee-for-service rate

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of Health and Human Services shall provide, beginning with 2005, for adjustment of payment rates to organizations under section 1853 of the Social Security Act so that such payment rates reflect only the payment rate described in subsection (c)(1)(D) of such section (relating to 100 percent fee-for-service payment).

## § 4. Application of risk adjustment reflecting characteristics for the entire medicare population

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, effective January 1, 2005, in applying risk adjustment factors to payment to organizations under section 1853 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w–23) in a budget neutral manner, the Secretary of Health and Human Services shall assure that such factors, in the aggregate, take into account the actuarial characteristics of the entire medicare population, and not merely the population of individuals enrolled under a plan under part C of title XVIII of such Act.
