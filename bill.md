# H.R. 4299

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 410 South Jackson Road in Edinburg, Texas, as the “Dr. Miguel A. Nevarez Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Designation of Dr. Miguel A. Nevárez Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 410 South Jackson Road in Edinburg, Texas, shall be known and designated as the “Dr. Miguel A. Nevárez Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Dr. Miguel A Nevárez Post Office Building”.
