# H.R. 3851

> To authorize an additional permanent judgeship for the district of Hawaii.

## § 1. District judgeship for the district of Hawaii

- **(a)** *Additional permanent district judgeship* The President shall appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, 1 additional district judge for the district of Hawaii.
