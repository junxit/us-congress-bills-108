# H.R. 4305

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to increase protections for children from obscene material on the Internet.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “&short-title1;”.

## § 2. Increased protection for children from obscene material on the Internet

Subsections (b) and (c) of section 2252B of title 18, United States Code, are each amended by inserting “or meta tag” after “domain name”.
