# H.R. 5136

> To amend section 108 of title 17, United States Code, relating to reproduction of works by libraries and archives.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preservation of Orphan Works Act”.

## § 2. Reproduction of copyrighted works by libraries and archives

Section 108(i) of title 17, United States Code, is amended by striking “(b) and (c)” and inserting “(b), (c), and (h)”.
