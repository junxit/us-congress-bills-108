# H.R. 4244

> To designate the visitor center at the U.S.S. Arizona Memorial in Hawaii as the “Pearl Harbor Memorial Site Visitor Center”.

## § 1. Designation of Pearl Harbor Memorial Site Visitor Center

- **(a)** *Designation* The visitor center at the U.S.S. Arizona Memorial in Hawaii is hereby designated as the “Pearl Harbor Memorial Site Visitor Center”.
- **(b)** *Reference* Any reference to the visitor center at the U.S.S. Arizona Memorial in Hawaii, in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be considered to be a reference to the “Pearl Harbor Memorial Site Visitor Center”.
