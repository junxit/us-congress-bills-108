# H.R. 3923

> To designate the Federal building located at 228 Walnut Street, in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, as the “Ronald Reagan Federal Building”.

## § 1. Designation

The Federal building located at 228 Walnut Street, in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, shall be known and designated as the “Ronald Reagan Federal Building”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Ronald Reagan Federal Building”.
