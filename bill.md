# H.R. 5384

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make the allowance of the deduction of State and local general sales taxes in lieu of State and local income taxes permanent.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Permanent Sales Tax Deduction Act of 2004”.

## § 2. Deduction of State and local sales taxes made permanent

Paragraph (5) of section 164(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking subparagraph (I).
