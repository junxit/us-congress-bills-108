# H.R. 5180

> To extend the authority of the Livestock Mandatory Reporting Act of 1999 through October 22, 2005.

## § 1. Extension of the Livestock Mandatory Reporting Act of 1999 through October 22, 2005

Section 942 of the Livestock Mandatory Reporting Act of 1999 (7 U.S.C. 1635 note) is amended by striking “5 years” and inserting “6 years”.
