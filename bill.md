# H.R. 5157

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to expand the risk pools that qualify for high risk pool grants.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “High Risk Pool Flexibility Act of 2004”.

## § 2. Revision of definition of qualified high risk pool

Section 2745(d) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg–45(d)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(d)** *Definitions* For purposes of this section:
>   - **(1)** *Qualified high risk pool* The term ‘qualified high risk pool’ has the meaning given such term in section 2744(c)(2), except that a State may elect to meet the requirement of subparagraph (A) of such section (insofar as it requires the provision of coverage to all eligible individuals) through providing for the enrollment of eligible individuals through an acceptable alternative mechanism (as defined for purposes of section 2744) that includes a high risk pool as a component.
>   - **(2)** *State* The term State means any of the 50 States and the District of Columbia.
