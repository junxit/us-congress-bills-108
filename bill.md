# S. 2928

> To clarify the status of certain employee benefit plans under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 and the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974.

## § 1. Clarification of status of certain employee benefit plans

Any plan established and maintained for its employees by a non-profit corporation which was—

- **(1)** incorporated on September 16, 1998, under a State non-profit corporation statute, and
- **(2)** organized for the express purpose of supporting the missions and goals of a public corporation which—
  - **(A)** was created by a State statute approved by the Governor on May 24, 1995, and effective on July 1, 1995,
  - **(B)** is a government entity under State law, and
  - **(C)** is a member of the non-profit corporation,
