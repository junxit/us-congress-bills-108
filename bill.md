# H.R. 4189

> To modify and improve the funding structure of the Environmental Quality Incentives Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Full Enrollment Environmental Quality Incentives Program (FEEQIP) Act of 2004”.

## § 2. Funding of the Environmental Quality Incentives Program

Section 1241(a)(6) of the Food Security Act of 1985 (16 U.S.C. 3821(a)(6)) is amended by striking all that follows “chapter 4” and inserting a period.
