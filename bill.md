# H.R. 4489

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to mandate a life sentence for repeat sex offenders.

## § 1. Mandatory Life Sentence for Repeat Sex Offenders

Subsection (a) of section 2247 of title 18, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(a)** *Mandatory Life Sentence* The term of imprisonment for a violation of this chapter after a prior sex offense conviction shall be life.
