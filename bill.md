# H.R. 5424

> To repeal a provision relating to privacy officers in the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2005.

## § 1. Repeal of Privacy Officer provision

Section 522 of the Transportation, Treasury, Independent Agencies, and General Government Appropriations Act, 2005 (division H of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2005), is hereby repealed.
