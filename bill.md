# H.R. 3732

> To amend the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 to repeal the recently enacted two-year delay in the implementation of the country of origin labeling requirements of such Act for certain agricultural commodities.

## § 1. Repeal of delay in implementation of country of origin labeling requirements of Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946

Section 285 of the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 (7 U.S.C. 1638d), as amended by section 749 of the Agriculture, Rural Development, Food and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2004 (division A of Public Law 108–199), is amended by striking “September 30, 2006” and all that follows through the period at the end and inserting “September 30, 2004.”.
