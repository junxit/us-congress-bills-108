# H.R. 3924

> To authorize an annual appropriation of $10,000,000 for mental health courts through fiscal year 2009.

## § 1. Authorization of appropriations

Section 1001(a)(20) of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3793(a)(20)) is amended by striking “fiscal years 2001 through 2004” and inserting “fiscal years 2004 through 2009”.
