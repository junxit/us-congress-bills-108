# H.R. 3775

> To impose a ban on the importation of soybeans and soybean meal that are products of Argentina or Brazil.

## § 1. Ban on Importation of Soybeans and Soybean Meal that are Products of Argentina or Brazil

- **(a)** *In General* No person may import into the customs territory of the United States soybeans provided for in heading 1201.00.00 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States, or soybean meal provided for in subheading 1208.10.00 of such Schedule, that is the product of Argentina or Brazil.
- **(b)** *Construction* For purposes of subsection (a), the term “import” includes withdrawal from warehouse for consumption.
