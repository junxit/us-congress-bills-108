# H.R. 3713

> To designate the Federal building located at 250 West Cherry Street in Carbondale, Illinois the “Senator Paul Simon Federal Building”.

## § 1. Designation of Federal building

The Federal building located at 250 West Cherry Street in Carbondale, Illinois shall be known and designated as the “Senator Paul Simon Federal Building”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Senator Paul Simon Federal Building”.
