# H.R. 4065

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to increase the maximum amount of home loan guaranty available to a veteran, and to provide for annual adjustments to such amount.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “&short-title1;”.

## § 2. Increase in, and annual indexing of, maximum amount of home loan guaranty for construction and purchase of homes

- **(a)** *Maximum loan guaranty based on 90 percent of the Freddie Mac conforming loan rate* Section 3703(a)(1) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “$60,000” each place it appears in subparagraphs (A)(i)(IV) and (B) and inserting “the maximum guaranty amount (as defined in subparagraph (C))”.
- **(b)** *Definition* Such section is further amended by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(C)** In this paragraph, the term “maximum guaranty amount” means the dollar amount that is equal to 22.5 percent of the Freddie Mac conforming loan limit limitation determined under section 305(a)(2) of the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation Act (12 U.S.C. 1454(a)(2)), as adjusted for the year involved.
