# H.R. 5167

> To extend for 18 months the period for which chapter 12 of title 11 of the United States Code is reenacted.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protection of Family Farmers Act of 2004”.

## § 2. Eighteen-month extension of period for which chapter 12 of title 11, United States Code, is reenacted

- **(a)** *Amendments* Section 149 of title I of division C of Public Law 105–277 (11 U.S.C. 1201 note) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “January 1, 2004” each place it appears and inserting “July 1, 2005”; and
  - **(2)** in subsection (a)—
    - **(A)** by striking “June 30, 2003” and inserting “December 31, 2003”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “July 1, 2003” and inserting “January 1, 2004”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) take effect on January 1, 2004.
