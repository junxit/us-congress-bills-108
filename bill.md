# H.R. 389

> To authorize the use of certain grant funds to establish an information clearinghouse that provides information to increase public access to defibrillation in schools.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “&short-title1;”.

## § 2. Amendment to Public Health Service Act

Subsection (c) of section 312 of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 244), as amended by Public Law 107–188, is amended—

- **(1)** at the end of paragraph (5), by striking “and”;
- **(2)** by redesignating paragraph (6) as paragraph (7); and
- **(3)** by inserting after paragraph (5) the following:
  > - **(6)** establish an information clearinghouse that provides information to increase public access to defibrillation in schools; and
