# H.R. 4262

> To provide for earned adjustment to reward work, reunify families, establish a temporary worker program that protects United States and foreign workers and strengthen national security under the immigration laws of the United States.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as—

- **(1)** the “Safe, Orderly, Legal Visas and Enforcement Act of 2004”; or
- **(2)** the “&short-title1;”.
