# H.R. 4563

> To require the Secretary of the Treasury to redesign the face of $20 Federal reserve notes so as to include a likeness of President Ronald Wilson Reagan, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the “President Ronald Reagan $20 Bill Act”.

## § 2. Likeness of President Reagan Required to be Included on the Face of $20 Federal Reserve Notes

The 8th undesignated paragraph of section 16 of the Federal Reserve Act (12 U.S.C. 418) is amended by adding at the end the following new sentence: “The face of $20 Federal reserve notes printed after December 31, 2004, shall bear the likeness of President Ronald Wilson Reagan”.
