# H.R. 5268

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to require the National Transportation Safety Board to investigate all fatal railroad grade crossing accidents.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fatal Grade Crossing Accident Investigations Act”.

## § 2. Grade crossing accidents

Section 1131(a)(1)(C) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by inserting “, including a railroad grade crossing accident,” after “railroad accident”.
