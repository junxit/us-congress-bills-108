# H.R. 4823

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to permit foreign media representatives to gain admission as visitors coming temporarily to the United States for business.

## § 1. Removal of prohibition on admission of foreign media representatives as visitors

Section 101(a)(15)(B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(15)(B)) is amended by striking “or as a representative of foreign press, radio, film, or other foreign information media coming to engage in such vocation”.
