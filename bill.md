# H.R. 4073

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to treat payments under the Conservation Reserve Program as rentals from real estate.

## § 1. Treatment of conservation reserve program payments as rentals from real estate

- **(a)** *Internal Revenue Code* Section 1402(a)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (defining net earnings from self-employment) is amended by inserting “and including payments under section 1233(2) of the Food Security Act of 1985 (16 U.S.C. 3833(2))” after “crop shares”.
- **(b)** *Social Security Act* Section 211(a)(1) of the Social Security Act is amended by inserting “and including payments under section 1233(2) of the Food Security Act of 1985 (16 U.S.C. 3833(2))” after “crop shares”.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to payments made after the date of the enactment of this Act.
