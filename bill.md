# H.R. 4253

> To amend title 4, United States Code, to require the display at half-staff of the flag of the United States on National Workers Memorial Day.

## § 1. Display of Flag of the United States at Half-Staff on National Workers Memorial Day

Section 6(d) of title 4, United States Code, is amended by inserting before “Mother’s Day” the following: “National Workers Memorial Day (half-staff), April 28;”.
