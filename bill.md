# H.R. 5298

> To amend title 4, United States Code, to clarify the treatment of self-employment for purposes of the limitation on State taxation of retirement income.

## § 1. Clarification of treatment of self-employment for purposes of the limitation on State taxation of retirement income

- **(a)** *In general* Section 114(b)(1)(I) of title 4, United States Code, is amended by inserting “including such a plan, program, or arrangement for a self-employed individual,” after “section 3121(v)(2)(C) of such Code,”.
- **(b)** *Application* The amendment made by this section applies to amounts received after December 31, 1995.
