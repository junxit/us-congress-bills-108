# H.R. 3740

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 223 South Main Street in Roxboro, North Carolina, as the “Oscar Scott Woody Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Oscar Scott Woody Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 223 South Main Street in Roxboro, North Carolina, shall be known and designated as the “Oscar Scott Woody Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the Oscar Scott Woody Post Office Building.
