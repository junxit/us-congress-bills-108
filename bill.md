# H.R. 4922

> To amend the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to reauthorize programs to improve the quality of coastal recreation waters, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Coastal recreation water quality monitoring and notification

Section 406(i) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1346(i)) is amended by striking “2005” and inserting “2010”.

## § 2. Authorization of appropriations

Section 8 of the Beaches Environmental Assessment and Coastal Health Act of 2000 (114 Stat. 877) is amended by striking “2005” and inserting “2010”.
