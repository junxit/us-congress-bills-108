# H.R. 3884

> To designate the Federal building and United States courthouse located at 615 East Houston Street in San Antonio, Texas, as the “Hipolito F. Garcia Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

## § 1. Designation

The Federal building and United States courthouse located at 615 East Houston Street in San Antonio, Texas, shall be known and designated as the “Hipolito F. Garcia Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building and United States courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Hipolito F. Garcia Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.
